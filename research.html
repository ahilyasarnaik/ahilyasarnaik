Research

Research interests and findings

Federated learning for anomaly detection

Investigating adaptive aggregation under heterogeneous network-traffic partitions using NSL-KDD and UNSW-NB15. In the reported experiments, adaptive aggregation did not consistently improve NSL-KDD results, but showed an improvement on UNSW-NB15 under high heterogeneity (α = 0.1). Results are dataset- and configuration-dependent.

Adaptive cyber deception

Exploring lightweight honeypots that vary exposed signatures and response behaviour to make simple fingerprinting less reliable, while measuring resource cost and operational tradeoffs.

Malware anti-forensics

Studying how malware manipulates Windows Registry artifacts and how evolving automation may complicate forensic reconstruction.
